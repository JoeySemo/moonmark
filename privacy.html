Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026 This policy covers the Maasverse Reading Companion iOS app ("the app"). The app is an unofficial, fan-made reading companion. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Sarah J. Maas, Bloomsbury, or any other rights holder.

What the app collects

Your place in each book. The chapter you have reached in each book, and which book you last opened. That is the whole point of the app: it decides what you are allowed to see, so that nothing spoils a book you have not finished. An anonymous account id. So your progress survives reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone, the app signs you in anonymously and the server issues a random account id. It identifies the installation, not you. Signing in requires no email address, no password and no name. That is everything. The app does not collect or ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, health data, payment details, device advertising identifier, or any other personal information.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, as Apple defines tracking.
  • Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with other companies or data brokers.
  • No profiling and no automated decisions about you.

Where the data is stored

Your reading position is stored:

  • On your device, so the app and its Home Screen widget work offline. It is

    • held in the app's own storage, shared between the app and the widget.

  • On the app's server, hosted by Supabase, which

    • stores it on our behalf as a processor. Rows are protected so that only your own anonymous account can read or write your progress.

Progress is sent to the server only when a session is available. If the server cannot be reached, the app keeps working from the copy on your device.

How long it is kept

Your progress is kept until you delete it, or until the account has been inactive for two years, after which it may be removed.

Deleting your data

  • On the device: delete the app. That removes the local copy, including the

    • widget's snapshot.

  • On the server: open the app, tap the info button on the Progress tab, and

    • choose Delete my backup. The backup is tied to an anonymous account id with no contact details attached, so it cannot be looked up by name or email — which is exactly why deletion is a button in the app rather than an email request.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data, and to complain to a data protection authority. The only data held is the reading position and anonymous account id described above. The app itself shows you that data (it is your place in each book) and deletes it on request through the About screen.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the current version is published at the same URL.

Contact

Deletion does not need a contact: use Delete my backup in the app's About screen. For anything else, use the support contact shown on the app's App Store listing.

Maasverse Reading Companion is unofficial and fan-made, not affiliated with or endorsed by any author or publisher.
